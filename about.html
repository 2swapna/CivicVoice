About CivicVoice

CivicVoice is a community-focused platform that allows citizens to report and track local problems in their area.

The platform is designed to make it easier for citizens to communicate civic issues and follow their progress.

The Problem We Solve

Local problems such as potholes, garbage overflow, broken streetlights, water leakage and drainage issues can affect the community.

CivicVoice provides a central platform where these issues can be reported, tracked and managed.

How CivicVoice Works

  1. Report: A citizen reports a local problem.
  2. Review: The admin reviews the reported problem.
  3. Assign: The complaint is assigned to the appropriate department.
  4. Track: The citizen can track the progress of the complaint.
  5. Resolve: The status is updated when the problem is resolved.

Community Participation

Citizens can view public problems, support issues and participate through comments. This helps highlight problems that affect multiple people in the community.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a simple and transparent platform where citizens can report civic problems and follow their progress from reporting to resolution.
